Props are CC0 1.0 (public domain) low-poly models from Quaternius and Kenney,
via the npm package @netforge/asset-library-files. Restyled at runtime (toon shading, ink outlines, recolour).

chars/ (used only in the peek-through-the-gate scene):
 - Soldier.glb, Xbot.glb: Mixamo characters as bundled in the three.js examples (free for use in projects).
 - RobotExpressive.glb: Tomas Laulhe (CC0), via three.js examples.
 - CesiumMan.glb: Cesium (CC BY 4.0), via KhronosGroup glTF-Sample-Assets.
 - fox.glb: Quaternius animals (CC0).

arts/ (collectible artifacts in the peek scene; textures downscaled):
 - lamp.glb (IridescenceLamp): Wayfair, CC BY 4.0, via KhronosGroup glTF-Sample-Assets.
 - helmet.glb (DamagedHelmet): 'Battle Damaged Sci-fi Helmet' by theblueturtle_, CC BY 4.0.
 - amber.glb (MosquitoInAmber): model by Loic Norgeot, mosquito scan by Geoffrey Marchal (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0.
 - drone and obelisk reuse robot_flyer.glb and obelisk.glb above (CC0).
